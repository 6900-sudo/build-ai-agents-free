# Ready Ground — 90-Day Launch Checklist

A week-by-week plan for the **recommended pathway**: an audience-and-affiliate flywheel in a named sub-niche — content (video-first) → email list → affiliate commissions + your own digital products. This is the path the [viability report](./viability-and-earnings-2026.md) recommends over physical dropshipping.

**Read this first — the mindset for 90 days:**
- Months 1–3 are for **building the asset, not earning**. Judge success by audience and email growth, not revenue. Near-zero income in this window is the expected, normal outcome, not failure.
- Time budget assumed: **~15–25 hours/week**, solo, near-zero paid ads.
- The one number that matters at day 90: **email subscribers**. Everything else feeds it.
- Keep all language "emergency / preparedness / home-resilience" — never "tactical / combat / self-defence" — to stay advertisable and processor-safe.

---

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Days 1–14)

**Goal: decide who you're for, and set up the plumbing so you never touch it again.**

### Strategy
- [ ] **Pick ONE sub-niche and commit.** Choose from: urban/apartment preparedness · family-with-kids prep · medical-continuity prep (chronic conditions) · UK/EU home-resilience (72-hour-kit angle). Write a one-line positioning statement: *"Ready Ground helps [who] get [outcome] without [pain]."*
- [ ] Define your **audience avatar** — one specific person (age, living situation, what worries them, what they've already tried).
- [ ] List **10 content pillars** (recurring topics) the niche cares about. These become your video/article backlog.

### Brand & tech (keep it minimal)
- [ ] Lock the brand name/handle and check it's free across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and a domain.
- [ ] Buy the domain (~£10/yr). Don't build a big site yet — a one-page site is enough.
- [ ] Stand up a **free email platform** (beehiiv or Kit free tier) and create one signup form.
- [ ] Create social accounts (YouTube + TikTok minimum; Instagram optional). Fill out bios pointing to the email lead magnet.
- [ ] Set up a simple link-in-bio (beehiiv hosted page, Carrd, or Stan) pointing to the lead-magnet signup.

### Legal/finance (light-touch — don't over-invest pre-revenue)
- [ ] Decide structure: sole trader is fine to start; form a **Ltd** once you have revenue or real liability exposure. (No physical goods yet = low liability.)
- [ ] Open a separate bank account/pot for the business so money is clean from day one.
- [ ] Note the thresholds you're **not** near yet but must watch: UK VAT at £90k turnover; US state economic nexus (~$100k/200 transactions per state) only matters once you sell physical goods into the US.

**Phase 0 done when:** sub-niche chosen, accounts live, email form working, lead magnet decided.

## Phase 1 — First content + lead magnet (Days 15–30)

**Goal: publish the free lead magnet and start posting. Get the flywheel turning.**

### The lead magnet (your email-list engine)
- [ ] Build the **free lead magnet** — the *72-Hour Blackout Checklist* you already have (in the Blackout Ready kit) is ideal. Export as a clean one-page PDF.
- [ ] Put it behind the email signup. Test the full flow yourself: sign up → receive email → download works.
- [ ] Write a **3–5 email welcome sequence** (delivered automatically): email 1 delivers the checklist; 2–5 give genuine value and softly introduce your paid guide later.

### Content production (video-first)
- [ ] Turn the **5 Blackout Ready scripts** into 5 short-form videos (9:16, calm voice, subtitles, "AI-generated" label ticked). You already have the scripts.
- [ ] Publish **3–4 videos/week** across YouTube Shorts + TikTok. Every caption/end-card points to the free checklist.
- [ ] Write **1 buyer-intent article/week** on your one-page site or a blog ("best power bank for a flat", "72-hour kit for a family of four") — commercial-intent content survives AI Overviews better than pure how-to.
- [ ] Batch-record: sit down once and record 2 weeks of videos at a time. Consistency beats polish.

**Phase 1 done when:** lead magnet live, welcome sequence running, first 8–12 pieces of content published.

## Phase 2 — Monetization rails (Days 31–60)

**Goal: put the money-making pieces in place so traffic can convert — without waiting for scale.**

### Affiliate (no inventory, no fulfillment)
- [ ] Apply to the **high-ticket, long-cookie affiliate programs**: ReadyWise (10%, 120-day cookie), 1800Prepare (10%, 120-day), LifeStraw (via Impact). Also Amazon Associates for breadth.
- [ ] **4Patriots affiliate** — runs on the **Awin** network, **30-day cookie**. The **commission rate is not published and is negotiable** per partner (secondary listicles quote ~10–15% / up to $50–450 per sale, but that is unverified — confirm your actual rate inside Awin after approval). High AOV (~$232 avg, generators in the hundreds) makes even a modest % worthwhile. Apply via the 4Patriots affiliate page / Awin. _(Sources: [linkclicky](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/4patriots/), [shopday](https://www.shopday.com/4patriots))_
- [ ] Add **honest affiliate recommendations** into your best articles and video descriptions — "the gear I'd actually buy" lists. Disclose affiliate links (FTC + ASA require it).
- [ ] Feature the **closed flagship brands** (Jackery, EcoFlow, LifeStraw) via affiliate for credibility — you earn on the recommendation without needing a dealer account.

### Your own digital product (highest margin, ~90%)
- [ ] Build **product #1**: the *Blackout Ready: Full Guide* you already drafted → export to PDF, list on **Gumroad** (flat 10% fee) at £5–9. Both Gumroad and Etsy handle UK/EU VAT for you.
- [ ] Design a simple cover and 3 preview images.
- [ ] Plan **2 more digital products** (a printable home-inventory sheet; a family emergency-plan template). Multi-product sellers earn far more than single-product ones — aim for 3+.

### Measurement
- [ ] Add basic analytics (the platform's native stats + a free tool). Track the ONE metric weekly: **email subscribers**, plus video views and top-performing topics.

**Phase 2 done when:** affiliate links live in content, digital product #1 on sale, analytics tracked weekly.

## Phase 3 — Optimize & compound (Days 61–90)

**Goal: double down on what's working, kill what isn't, and set up the review gate.**

- [ ] **Review your data:** which 2–3 content topics drove the most views and signups? Make more of exactly those. Drop the formats that flopped.
- [ ] Ship **digital products #2 and #3**; cross-link them from the first.
- [ ] Send a **weekly email** to your list (value + occasional soft product/affiliate mention). The list is the asset that survives any algorithm change.
- [ ] Add a **"start here" content hub** on the site linking your best pieces, each routing to the lead magnet.
- [ ] Repurpose winners: turn a top Short into a longer YouTube video and an article, and vice-versa.
- [ ] **(Optional) Physical goods test — only if content is clearly working.** If and only if you want to add physical products: start with a **mid-ticket bundle** ($50–90 AOV, ~55% gross) via a **US-warehoused aggregator** (Spocket or CJ), price **DDP** (duties included at checkout), buy **product-liability insurance with US territorial cover**, and keep copy "emergency/preparedness". Do not touch tactical, knives, or self-defence SKUs (processor-freeze / MATCH-list risk).

### The 90-day review gate (do this on day 90)
- [ ] Tally the real numbers: email subscribers, total content pieces, best channel, any revenue.
- [ ] **Decide with these rules of thumb:**
  - **Continue** if email list and views are growing steadily month-on-month — even at near-zero revenue. That's the asset compounding on schedule.
  - **Adjust** (change sub-niche angle or format, not the model) if content is published consistently but nothing is landing.
  - **Stop** only if you couldn't sustain consistent publishing — the model needs 12+ months and consistency is the single biggest predictor of who makes it.

## What "on track" looks like at day 90

| Metric | Conservative | Realistic | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email subscribers | 100–300 | 300–800 | 1,000+ |
| Content published | ~30 pieces | ~40 pieces | 50+ |
| Digital products live | 1 | 2–3 | 3+ |
| Revenue | ~£0–50/mo | ~£50–200/mo | £300+/mo |

Revenue is deliberately last. Per the report, a realistic month-**12** run-rate is ~£1,500/mo net — the first 90 days is about building the audience that gets you there, not the income itself.

---

## The non-negotiables (don't skip these)

1. **Consistency over polish** — publishing 3–4x/week for 90 days beats one perfect video.
2. **Original scripting** — YouTube demonetizes templated "AI slop" channel-wide; keep a distinctive voice.
3. **No fake reviews/testimonials** — FTC bans AI-generated "verified buyer" social proof (up to $51,744/violation).
4. **Disclose affiliate links** and keep every safety claim factually defensible (cite FDA/Ready.gov/CDC as the Blackout Ready kit already does).
5. **The email list is the business** — an algorithm change can halve your reach overnight; the list can't be taken from you.
